# Synthetic demonstration data

Every record generated here is fictional. `python scripts/generate_sample_data.py` uses seed 42 and creates 24 months of Asteria Retail Labs data.

Injected patterns: November/December revenue seasonality; regional mix differences; varied price/margin profiles; repeat customers; discount bands; product return variation; a controlled Black-Friday-week spike; 1% missing customer age groups; ten duplicate sessions; and five deliberately lower-case region labels. These are designed for profiling and anomaly demonstrations, not causal inference.
